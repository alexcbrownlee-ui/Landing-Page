05 / The Campaign

Seven days. Seven emails. Each one has a job.

  1. Day 1
    Announcement

    What's opening, who it's for, why now.

  2. Day 2
    Story

    Why you coach the way you do.

  3. Day 3
    Proof

    A real client result, with permission.

  4. Day 4
    Objections

    "No time." "Tried before." Answered.

  5. Day 5
    Value

    A tip worth reading, even if they don't buy.

  6. Day 6
    Urgency

    The deadline, stated plainly.

  7. Day 7
    Last Call

    Short, calm, final.

Every deadline is real. If doors close Friday, they close Friday.
